Example Devices Corp. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

AUSTIN, Texas, November 4, 2026 - Example Devices Corp. (NASDAQ: SAMPLE), a maker of consumer electronics and related services, today announced financial results for its fiscal 2026 third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
- Total net sales of $165.5 million, an increase of 13.1% from the prior-year period
- Operating income of $18.9 million
- Net income of $14.2 million and diluted earnings per share of $1.42
- Services revenue of $45.1 million, an increase of 14.6% from the prior-year period

"We delivered another strong quarter," said the Chief Executive Officer of Example Devices Corp. "Sales growth was driven by record demand for our new devices and a 14.6% increase in services revenue, while net income and diluted earnings per share included a one-time gain of $6.0 million from the sale of a legacy patent portfolio, which we do not expect to recur."

Fourth Quarter 2026 Outlook
The company is raising its outlook. We now expect fourth quarter total net sales of approximately $170 million, up from our prior expectation of approximately $160 million, reflecting strong early holiday demand.
